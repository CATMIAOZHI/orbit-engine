# instrumentation harness, so the suite runs on a consumer's classpath.

# FirebaseMessaging.getToken() hangs with NoClassDefFoundError on
# PreferencesKeys. Its okio-jvm is the only okio: slim_aar.py strips the
# orphaned one from the AAR, so do not exclude okio here.
